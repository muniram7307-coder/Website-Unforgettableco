# Unforgettableco

Website for **Unforgettableco** — a photobooth hire business for weddings, birthday parties, and experiential retail activations.

**Live site:** https://muniram7307-coder.github.io/Website-Unforgettableco/

## About the business

Unforgettableco provides photobooth experiences for private celebrations and brand-led retail moments. Guests get instant prints and shareable digital keepsakes; hosts and retailers get an engaging, memorable touchpoint for their event or store.

## Proposed site structure

A lean sitemap for a small hire business — enough to convert enquiries without overwhelming maintenance.

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Home** | Hero, what you offer, event types (weddings, birthdays, retail), social proof snippet, primary CTA to enquire |
| **Packages** | Booth options, hire durations, what's included (prints, props, backdrop, attendant, digital gallery), starting prices |
| **Gallery** | Photos and short clips from past events — builds trust and shows the experience in action |
| **Retail & activations** | Dedicated pitch for shops, malls, and pop-ups: footfall, dwell time, branded overlays, lead capture |
| **About** | Brand story, why "Unforgettableco", team, service area, and what makes your setup different |
| **FAQ** | Space requirements, setup/breakdown time, power needs, guest capacity, weather/back-up plans |
| **Contact / Book** | Enquiry form (date, venue, event type, package), email/phone, response-time expectation |

### Optional later

- **Testimonials** — standalone page or a section on Home once you have enough reviews
- **Blog / events journal** — recap posts for SEO and social sharing
- **Custom branding** — for corporate and retail clients wanting logo overlays and branded prints
