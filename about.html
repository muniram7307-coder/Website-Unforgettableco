About us

We believe the best events give people something to take home — a laugh, a memory, and a photo on the fridge.

Why Unforgettableco?

We started Unforgettableco because we saw how a simple photobooth could transform an event. The queue forms, the props come out, and suddenly even the shyest guest is laughing in front of the camera.

That energy is what we bring to every hire — whether it's a wedding reception, a 40th birthday, or a weekend activation in a busy shopping centre.

We're a small, hands-on team. When you book with us, you get people who care about the details: the lighting, the backdrop, the guest experience, and the moment the first print comes off the machine.

What we stand for

Reliable

We arrive on time, set up without fuss, and run the booth smoothly from first guest to last.

Personal

Every event is different. We listen to what you need and tailor the setup to match.

Professional

Quality equipment, polished prints, and a friendly attendant who represents your event well.

Fun

At the heart of it, we're here to help people have a brilliant time. That's what makes it unforgettable.
